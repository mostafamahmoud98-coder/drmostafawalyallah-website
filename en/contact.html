Before your visit

  • Please bring any previous reports or investigations (OCT, topography, old glasses prescription, etc.).
  • If you wear contact lenses, plan to remove them well before the consultation (we'll tell you how long, based on lens type).
  • The examination may include pupil dilation — an accompanying person is recommended, as driving afterwards is uncomfortable.

A note on urgent cases

For urgent symptoms (sudden loss of vision, a shower of floaters with flashes, a curtain in the vision, severe eye pain) — please message us directly on WhatsApp and note that it is urgent.
